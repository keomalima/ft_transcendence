Case 1 - Player 1 wins the match agains player 2
- All players get the news to update the brackelist (match won and next match)
- Player 1 gets the next match updated to the next opponent or the empty next match card
- Player 2 gets the you lost card

Case 2 - Player 1 quits tournament
- All players get the news to update the brackelist (match won and next match)
- Player 1 gets the next match updated to the next opponent or the empty next match card

Case 3 - Player 1 wins the tournament 
- All players get the news to update the brackelist (match won)
- All players display 'player 1 won the tournament'
